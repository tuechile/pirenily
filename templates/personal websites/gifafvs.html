Physical & electronic art · October 2024

Out

Out was my first physical and electronic arts piece, made for the same class. I really like this piece for some reason, but my professor hated it. Technically, it was very unimpressive, but I think it holds a lot of meaning; I can think of five different readings of it right now.

Because the project was technically unimpressive, I got an A-. Out and What Brings You Here are the two pieces I display on the landing page.

Interactive · Processing (Java) · October 2024

What Brings You Here

This piece was my first ever homework submission at Harvard. It was created using Processing, a Java-based programming language that accommodates interactive visual arts. I wanted to create an input-based program that suggests different activities for other first-year college students, given the plethora of options available, just like the colors of candies in a jar.

There is a sense of randomness and surprise, which might seem contradictory to the meticulous planning that most students do when thinking about their future.

This project is supposed to be interactive. Here, I have included a gif of what it looks like when different inputs are entered.

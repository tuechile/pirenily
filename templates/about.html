Right now I am Director of Design & Engineering at the Harvard Undergraduate Hackathon (HackHarvard), a project manager at Tech for Social Good (T4SG), Director of Internal Relations at HPAIR, and a student content creator at the Office for the Arts.

My passion for film and installation art grows within my identity: pirenily. I use pirenily as a vessel of self-expression through graphic design, photography, and storyboards. My main interests lie in the construction of interdependence: of the movement of time within the static, of the existence of the multiverse through doors-and-paths-related design, and of myself as a locus of socialization.
